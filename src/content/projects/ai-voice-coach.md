As founding engineer at MakeTimeFlow, I architected and developed the company's core product: a voice-enabled AI coaching agent that helps professionals manage their time and focus through natural conversation.

The agent integrates Claude for reasoning and conversation management with ElevenLabs' real-time speech API for voice interaction. It maintains persistent user context and session state across multi-turn conversations, allowing it to reference previous coaching sessions, track commitments, and adapt its approach based on each user's patterns.

This feature became the product's core differentiator after resonating strongly in paid user interviews. The positive response drove a strategic pivot — the team shifted from a general-purpose SaaS tool toward AI-native positioning built around the voice coaching experience.

Beyond the AI agent, I own the full product lifecycle on our two-person engineering team: customer research, product strategy, architecture, and deployment. I drove the pivot from $30/month consumer subscriptions to $3,000 enterprise contracts based on insights from user research, and I re-architected the calendar integration system to support real-time webhook sync across multiple providers with an integration-agnostic service layer.
